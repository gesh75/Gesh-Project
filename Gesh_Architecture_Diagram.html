Funding Rate
>+0.10% → BEARISH (longs over-leveraged). <-0.05% → BULLISH (squeeze fuel). Contrarian signal.
Open Interest
OI↑ + price↑ → BULLISH. OI↑ + price↓ → BEARISH. New conviction vs short accumulation.
CVD Divergence
CVD↑ + price flat → BULLISH hidden accumulation. Tracks cumulative volume delta.
Options Flow
P/C >0.70 = bearish hedging. IV skew >5% = tail risk buying. Whale net flow direction.
